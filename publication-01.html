Publication corrections

Chirp parameter selection for affine frequency division multiplexing with MMSE equalization

Zunqi Li, C. Zhang, G. Song, X. Fang, X. Sha, Dirk Slock · IEEE Transactions on Communications 73(7), 5079–5093

  1. Correction item 1 · Technical clarification

    Location

    Page 2, Section I.B, “Comparison of AFDM, OTFS and OTSM.”

    Original statement

    “Another common feature among these modulations is their ability to separate channel path components based on the delay and Doppler spread, leading to optimal maximum likelihood (ML) detection performance [13], [14].”

    Clarification

    The statement concerns effective diversity rather than the classical asymptotic diversity order. For a sufficiently large number of transmitted symbols, often represented by a large frame size MN, and sufficiently large QAM constellations, OTFS can practically achieve full effective diversity when the channel paths are resolvable in the delay–Doppler domain [1].

    A suitable phase-rotation operation is required to obtain strict full delay–Doppler diversity in the setting studied by Surabhi et al. [2]. In AFDM, the corresponding phase rotation is embedded in the AFT/DAFT through its chirp-dependent phase factors [3].

    References

    1. Raviteja, P., Hong, Y., Viterbo, E., et al., “Effective diversity of OTFS modulation,” IEEE Wireless Communications Letters, vol. 9, no. 2, pp. 249–253, 2019.
    2. Surabhi, G. D., Augustine, R. M., and Chockalingam, A., “On the diversity of uncoded OTFS modulation in doubly-dispersive channels,” IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, vol. 18, no. 6, pp. 3049–3063, Jun. 2019.
    3. Bemani, A., Ksairi, N., and Kountouris, M., “AFDM: A full diversity next generation waveform for high mobility communications,” 2021 IEEE International Conference on Communications Workshops (ICC Workshops), 2021, doi: 10.1109/ICCWorkshops50388.2021.9473655.
