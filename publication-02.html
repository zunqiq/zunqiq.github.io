Publication corrections

A hybrid carrier communication system based on weighted affine Fourier transform

Zunqi Li, L. Ou, C. Zhang, X. Fang, X. Sha · IEEE Communications Letters 28(7), 1629–1633

  1. Correction item 1 · Technical clarification

    Location

    Discussion of the equivalent channel and the dependence of the error-rate performance on the affine Fourier transform parameters.

    Original statement

    Reference [8] explains from the perspective of the equivalent channel that the error-rate performance of the transmitted AFT signal is only related to p1 and independent of p2.

    Clarification

    Under LMMSE detection, p2 does not affect the detection performance, as shown in [R1]. Under ML detection, however, p2 affects the detection performance [R2].

    Additional references

    1. [R1] Zunqi Li, Chuanbin Zhang, Ge Song, Xiaojie Fang, Xuejun Sha, and Dirk Slock, “Chirp parameter selection for affine frequency division multiplexing with MMSE equalization,” IEEE Transactions on Communications, early access Dec. 2024; vol. 73, no. 7, pp. 5079–5093, Jul. 2025.
    2. [R2] Ali Bemani, Nassar Ksairi, and Marios Kountouris, “Affine frequency division multiplexing for next generation wireless communications,” IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, 2023, doi: 10.1109/TWC.2023.3260906.
